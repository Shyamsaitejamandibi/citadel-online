# Original artwork

All illustrations were generated with the **built-in image-generation tool**. No CLI/API fallback or third-party board-game artwork was used. The finished assets are saved in `public/art/` and consumed locally; the app does not call an image-generation service.

- `citadel-city.png` / `citadel-city.webp`: city hero painting.
- `characters.png` / `characters.webp`: portrait source sheet.
- `character-1.webp` through `character-8.webp`: individual portraits, extracted from the source sheet without stretching.
- `districts-common.webp` and `districts-special.webp`: district source sheets.
- `district-0.webp` through `district-27.webp`: individual district illustrations in catalog order.

Sharp was used only for deterministic sprite extraction and WebP optimization. The original generation prompts follow.
